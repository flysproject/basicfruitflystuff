      Welcome to the FlyBrain Sandbox

      An autonomous Drosophila driven by simulated neuromodulators, a mushroom-body memory and a region-level connectome — rendered inside a real environment you can drop stimuli into.

      1. Watch it decide. The fly is already running. The chip at the top-left shows its current behaviour; the brain at the top-right shows which neuropils are driving it, lit from the real JRC2018Unisex volume.
      2. Drop some food. Pick Rotten banana from the toolbelt and click anywhere in the scene. The fly follows the odour plume — casting when it loses the scent — and feeds once it arrives.
      3. Startle it. Drop a Fly swatter. Looming in the optic lobe fires the giant fibre and you will see an escape takeoff in the event log.
      4. Change its brain. Drag the Octopamine slider and watch flight readiness, wing-beat pitch and escape threshold all move together.

      Everything here is an approximation, and the app says which parts are which: the volumes are real, the ROI ellipsoids are seeded from anatomy and snapped to real tissue, and the connectome weights are qualitative unless you load your own.
